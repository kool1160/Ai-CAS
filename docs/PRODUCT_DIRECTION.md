# AI-CAS Product Direction

## Product identity

AI-CAS is a guided Corrective Action System for manufacturing and operations.
Its principle is `Standardize to Optimize`, with the product rule: simple in
front, powerful underneath.

AI-CAS helps operators and manufacturing teams capture an issue, confirm
source information, organize correction context, generate professional draft
material, preserve evidence, and route the result for human review.

## Users

The primary users are shop-floor operators, welders, fabricators,
supervisors, manufacturing and process engineers, quality personnel,
maintenance personnel, and production coordinators.

The workflow must remain useful on phone, iPad, and desktop, with priority on
fast floor-side capture.

## Core workflow

1. Capture Issue
2. Extract and Confirm
3. Build Correction
4. Generate Draft
5. Confirm and Save, Send, or Release through the applicable human gate

AI may structure and draft. A human confirms the facts and controls important
actions.

## Product boundary

AI-CAS is not an ERP, MES, quality-certification authority, unattended email
agent, automatic release authority, or generic analytics platform. New
backend, authentication, billing, customer-data, or enterprise workflow
scope requires an explicit product decision.

The existing product shell is preserved. Governance work must not become a
visual redesign or a navigation rewrite.

## Historical identity

Refab Connect and AI-WOC are historical identities. Existing references may be
retained when they explain provenance or compatibility, but active product
direction, new governance, and new user-facing work use AI-CAS.

## Quality bar

AI-CAS favors reliable, clear, safe, maintainable, scalable, usable, secure,
and evidence-backed behavior over speed or feature volume.
