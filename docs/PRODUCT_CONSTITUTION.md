# AI-CAS Product Constitution

1. **The operator experience stays simple.** Capture, confirm, correct,
   draft, review, and controlled action remain understandable.
2. **Human judgment owns important actions.** Sending, authoritative saving,
   release, deletion, publication, deployment, and external actions require
   explicit human confirmation.
3. **AI output is draft evidence, not truth.** AI must not invent source data,
   root causes, approvals, measurements, or release status.
4. **Source evidence remains attributable.** Extracted fields, manual edits,
   evidence metadata, ownership, and uncertainty must remain distinguishable.
5. **The system fails clearly.** Missing configuration, low confidence,
   invalid records, and unavailable services must produce a safe fallback.
6. **Private data stays out of public automation.** Customer, employer,
   personal, proprietary, and sensitive manufacturing data must not enter
   public tests, prompts, fixtures, logs, or CI.
7. **Secrets stay outside source control.** API keys, PINs, credentials, and
   production environment values must never be committed.
8. **Persistence claims must match evidence.** Browser-local records,
   validation stubs, and planned backends must not be described as durable
   authoritative storage.
9. **The product is not an ERP.** Routing and records work must serve the
   corrective-action workflow and not create an unrelated enterprise suite.
10. **Historical material cannot silently redefine the product.** FXD,
    Refab Connect, and AI-WOC references must be labeled by role and era.
11. **One milestone produces one reviewable result.** Scope, evidence,
    disagreements, assumptions, and unresolved risks are recorded.
12. **No automation bypasses approval.** Foreman automation may prepare,
    validate, and propose; it may not merge, deploy, rename, delete, or
    publish without explicit approval.
