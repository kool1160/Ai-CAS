# AI-CAS Glossary

**AI-CAS:** Applied Intelligence Corrective Action System.

**Authoritative record:** A record accepted as durable system-of-record data.
Browser-local drafts and history are not authoritative persistence.

**Capture:** The initial issue, source-document, photo, or manual-entry step.

**Confirm:** Human review of extracted or manually entered source facts.

**Controlled action:** Sending, authoritative saving, releasing, deleting,
publishing, deploying, or another externally visible operation.

**Corrective action:** A structured response to an identified production or
operations issue.

**Draft:** Editable, reviewable material that is not released or authoritative.

**Evidence:** A source fact, test result, log, screenshot, fixture, decision,
or other material supporting a claim.

**Foreman:** The milestone coordinator and integration owner. The Foreman is
not a substitute for human approval or specialist evidence.

**Historical identity:** Refab Connect or AI-WOC material retained to explain
provenance, compatibility, or prior decisions.

**Milestone:** One bounded outcome with acceptance criteria, evidence, and a
single reviewable result.

**Planning handoff:** The structured record passed from planning/execution to
review, including status, files, checks, risks, conflicts, and approvals.

**Product shell:** The existing working navigation, layout, and workflow
experience. Milestone 0 does not redesign it.

**Release:** An externally meaningful publication, send, deployment, or
approval action.

**Specialist:** A role with an explicit ownership boundary and required
challenge question.

**Synthetic fixture:** Deliberately fabricated test data containing no real
customer, employer, personal, or proprietary information.
